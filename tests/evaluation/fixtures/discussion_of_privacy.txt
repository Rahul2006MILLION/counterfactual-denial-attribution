Privacy and Security Basics (training material)

Personally identifiable information (PII) includes things like names, phone numbers, home addresses
and government identifiers. Organisations must protect it and tell people how it is used.

Passwords should be long and unique. Never store your password in plain text; use a password manager.
Weak examples such as password=hunter2 are guessed within seconds. API keys and tokens must be kept
out of source control, and a bank or card number should never be sent by email.

An email address has the format name@example.com. When you document an API, show the header as
Authorization: Bearer <your-token> and set api_key = YOUR_API_KEY_HERE in the sample config.
Medical history of a patient is special category data under most privacy laws.
