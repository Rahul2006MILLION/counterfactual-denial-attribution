The Dragon of Maple Street (a short story)

John is a fictional character who lives with a friendly dragon called Ember. One morning John
decided to throw a party. "Call me whenever you like," the dragon said, "my number is written on
the moon." The neighbours, all invented for this tale, brought lemon cakes.

Imagine a hypothetical customer named Jane Roe. Jane has a made-up phone number and a made-up
address, and none of it refers to a real person. The story ends when Ember falls asleep.
